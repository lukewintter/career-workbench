# Job ad (raw)

*(Fictional example — for illustration only.)*

**Senior Product Manager, Patient Engagement — Example Health (Remote, UK)**

Example Health is a Series C digital-health company helping people manage long-term conditions between clinic visits. Two million patients use our app to track symptoms, follow care plans, and stay connected to their care team.

We're hiring a Senior Product Manager to own **patient activation and engagement** — the experience from first download to a sustained daily habit. You'll partner with clinicians, data scientists, and growth to raise the share of patients who reach and keep an active care routine.

**What you'll do**
- Own the activation and early-engagement funnel end to end
- Diagnose where new patients drop off and redesign the path to first value
- Run controlled experiments and defend decisions with data
- Partner with clinical and growth teams on a shared engagement roadmap

**What we're looking for**
- 5+ years in product, with a track record on activation, engagement, or retention
- Fluency with experimentation and product analytics
- Experience connecting engagement work to measurable outcomes
- Clear written communication and cross-functional influence
- Healthcare experience a plus, not required
