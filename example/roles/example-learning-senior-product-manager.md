## Company context

Example Learning is a Series C edtech company (~180 people, Manchester HQ) selling a self-paced skills-learning platform to individual learners and, increasingly, to employers who buy team seats. Product-led growth on the consumer side; a growing B2B expansion motion on top.

## Mandate

Hired to own the learner onboarding and engagement surface — the path from sign-up to habitual use — and to make it measurably better. Later given the monetisation lever for the self-serve → team upgrade path.

## Scope

Two squads at peak (6 engineers, 1 designer, 1 data analyst). Owned activation, early retention, and self-serve→team expansion. Reported to the VP Product. Roadmap and experiment budget for the engagement area.

## Environment

Fast, evidence-led product org; weekly ship cadence; a shared experimentation platform. Cross-functional partners in growth marketing and customer success. Constraint: a legacy onboarding flow with heavy technical debt that shaped sequencing.
