# About me

*(Fictional example — Firstname Lastname.)*

## Positioning thesis

I'm a product manager who turns messy user problems into shipped, measured improvements. I'm strongest where activation, engagement, and monetisation meet — I dig into where users get stuck, redesign the path, and prove the change with data. I create the most leverage on products with a large top-of-funnel and a clear retention or expansion lever.

## Core strengths

- **Activation and engagement craft** — I find where new users drop off and rebuild the path; cut new-learner activation drop-off by 22 points at Example Learning (WS-001).
- **Commercial packaging** — I connect product decisions to revenue; introduced a team tier that grew expansion revenue 31% (WS-002).
- **Evidence-led decisions** — I instrument first, argue from data, and kill my own ideas when the numbers say so.

## Career narrative

I started in B2B SaaS at Example Software owning billing and pricing, where I learned to reason about monetisation without breaking trust. I moved to Example Learning (edtech) to work closer to end users, and my scope grew from a single onboarding surface to the whole learner-engagement journey. The through-line is the same problem in different clothes: reduce friction to the moment of value, then compound it. My next role is a lead/principal seat where I own a full growth or engagement area.

## Leadership and operating style

- **How I lead teams:** context over control — I make the goal and the constraints explicit, then let engineers and designers own the how.
- **How I make decisions:** instrument, form a hypothesis, run the smallest test that could disprove it.
- **How I handle ambiguity and trade-offs:** name the trade-off out loud, pick the reversible option, revisit on evidence.
- **How I work with executives and cross-functional peers:** short written narratives, one clear recommendation, the data behind it one click away.

## Product philosophy and values

Good product work is mostly subtraction — removing steps, not adding features. Strategy is a small number of bets you can actually resource. Execution quality shows up in the boring places (empty states, error paths, the second session). I'd rather ship a measured 5% improvement weekly than a speculative rewrite quarterly.

## Baseline voice and communication style

- **Tone:** direct, warm, concrete.
- **Writing style:** short sentences; lead with the recommendation; numbers in context.
- **Collaboration style:** think in the open, write things down, disagree early.
- **Words and phrases to avoid:** "synergy", "leverage" as a verb, "revolutionary", "world-class", vague superlatives.
