# Cover Letter Content

Promoted cover letter blocks for direct use in applications. Content

## Openings
*Use these as high-signal structures and examples. Reuse or adapt when
they fit; draft fresh when role/company context requires it. Keep the
opening tailored to the specific company and opportunity.*

### Direct hook
Lead with something specific from the job ad or company context.
Address their stated need immediately before introducing yourself.
Best for: roles with a very specific brief, or when you have an
obvious and direct match to call out.

*Example:*
Two of the areas you mentioned — [specific need from job ad] and
[second need] — are areas where I can contribute immediately.
Beyond my CV, here's a bit of context on both...

### Positioning statement
Lead with who you are and what you do well. Frame your positioning
thesis first, then connect it to the role.
Best for: broader roles, or when your overall profile is the strongest
argument for your candidacy.

```yaml
ws-refs: []
used-in: []
```
*Example:*
I'm an outcome-oriented senior PM who's strongest when there's
real-world messiness: multiple tracks in flight, imperfect information,
and the need to ship, learn, and adjust without losing sight of
customer impact...

### Role-specific framing
Lead with your most relevant experience for this specific role and
company context. Get specific about what you bring to this opportunity.
Best for: roles where one piece of your background is an unusually
strong match — lead with that.

```yaml
ws-refs: []
used-in: []
```
*Example:*
I'm a senior product manager currently working within [relevant domain].
For [Company]'s [specific initiative], I bring a track record of
[most relevant capability] — especially in [context where trust
or specific expertise matters]...
