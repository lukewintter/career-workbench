# reference-materials/

A staging area for career documents you **already have**. Drop them here and `setup-workbench` uses them as a starting point, so you're not building your workbench from a blank page.

Everything you drop here stays **local and uncommitted**: this folder's own `.gitignore` ignores everything in it except this README. Clear it whenever you're done.

## What to drop here

- Your **CV / résumé** — several, if you have meaningfully different versions
- **Cover letters** you've written before
- A **LinkedIn export** (or a copy of your profile)
- **Brag docs**, self-reviews, or performance reviews
- Anything else that records what you've done and how you've described it

## Formats

- **Markdown or plain text** work best.
- **PDF and .docx** work if your AI tool can read them. If it can't, paste the content into a `.md` or `.txt` file.

## How setup uses them

1. **Reads and sorts them first**, before writing anything: your positioning, the roles you've held, achievements, phrasing worth reusing, and CVs or letters worth keeping.
2. **Walks through each setup step with you** — config, role files, win stories, reusable assets — drafting from your documents and asking about everything they don't cover.
3. **Checks every claim with you.** Any number, outcome, or cause-and-effect claim in your documents comes back to you as a question before it's recorded. Nothing is invented or taken on trust.
4. **Writes nothing without your approval**, and finishes each step with you before starting the next.
5. **Keeps your best documents as references.** For each CV or cover letter you'd still send, setup offers to save a markdown copy. Future CVs and letters follow its length and voice, but never take claims from it. See [References](../README.md#references).

Your documents themselves stay here: setup writes new files in your workbench rather than copying these in. If this folder is empty, setup starts from scratch.
